# HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard is an interactive data visualization project developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze employee data and provide meaningful insights into workforce performance and HR operations.

The project focuses on transforming raw HR data into actionable insights that help HR professionals and management understand workforce distribution, employee satisfaction, promotion eligibility, and potential retrenchment risks.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze the overall workforce and employee distribution.
- Monitor employee promotion eligibility.
- Identify employees potentially at risk of retrenchment.
- Analyze employee satisfaction levels.
- Explore the relationship between overtime and workforce distribution.
- Compare HR indicators across departments and job roles.
- Support data-driven HR planning and decision-making.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Home – HR Overview

Provides a high-level overview of the workforce through key HR indicators and visualizations.

Key insights include:
- Total Employees
- Employee Status Distribution
- Gender Distribution
- Promotion Eligibility
- Workforce Distribution by Service Years
- Distance from Home Analysis

### 2. Action – Employee Action Analysis

Focuses on employee-level analysis to support HR actions and workforce planning.

Key insights include:
- Employees Due for Promotion
- Employees at Risk of Retrenchment
- Promotion and Retrenchment Analysis by Department
- Employee-Level Details for HR Follow-up

### 3. Details – Workforce Analysis

Provides a deeper analysis of employee characteristics and workforce patterns.

Key insights include:
- Workforce Distribution by Job Role
- Employee Distribution by Job Satisfaction Level
- Employee Distribution by Overtime Status
- Job Role Analysis
- Department-Level Workforce Insights
